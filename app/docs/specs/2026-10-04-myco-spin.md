# myco spin: turn the generated 360° turntable with the mouse

The procedural 3D myco (`2026-10-04-myco-3d.md`) reads as myco, but the krea turntables are more
faithful. They were made from GPT turnaround sheets with Seedance 2.5 (start frame = end frame =
front), and each covers exactly one turn: 6 s, 145 frames. The owner asked for a myco they can turn
with the mouse, so the card's main view becomes a spin viewer over those videos.

1. **Assets.**
   - `output/myco3d/spin-{black,white}.mp4` are copied to `app/src/assets/mascot/spin/`. They are
     640², all-intra H.264, 0.77 MB and 0.61 MB. Every frame is a keyframe, so seeking is exact.
   - Both are loaded lazily and only when the card is shown.
   - The motion-library video budget (≤ 4 MB of app videos) still holds; the budget test is updated.
2. **`MycoSpin` component.**
   - A `<video muted playsinline preload>`. Dragging horizontally maps to `currentTime`: a full
     drag across the element is one turn, and it wraps around both ends.
   - Release with inertia (exponential decay). The arrow keys turn it 15° per press, and Home goes
     back to the front.
   - Idle: a slow auto-turn after 4 s untouched, stopped under reduced motion.
   - States:
     - loading: the poster, the first frame;
     - error: the still pose;
     - ready.
   - The pure maps (`dragToTime`, `wrap`, `inertia`) are unit-tested.
3. **Settings card.**
   - The "myco 3D" card shows `MycoSpin` by default.
   - A small segment switches 영상 | 입체 (Video | 3D, en/ko/ja), keeping the procedural model as
     the second option.
   - The Black/White toggle drives both views.
