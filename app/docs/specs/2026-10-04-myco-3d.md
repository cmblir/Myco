# myco in 3D: black and white, turned with the mouse

Owner, 2026-10-04, with a screenshot of the near-black myco on a dark ground: "이런식의 완전 블랙 myco,
완전 화이트 myco 3d 형태 내가 마우스로 돌릴 수 있는 형태의 myco 영상을 만들어줘". The owner asked for the
image generation and sheets to be done with krea.ai and GPT. Those need the Chrome extension, which
is not connected, and Higgsfield has 0.8 credits. So this slice builds the 3D myco procedurally with
three.js, which is already a dependency. Generated reference sheets can refine it later.

## The character (unchanged identity, from the character sheet)

- A softly domed, slightly pointed cap with a slightly lighter underside rim.
- A short rounded body.
- Two white oval eyes.
- Small spherical orb hands, one raised beside the body as in the poses.
- No mouth, nose, limbs, outfit or text.
- Proportions are matched to `app/src/assets/mascot/poses/*.png`: compare silhouettes side by side.

## Decisions

1. **One procedural model, `lib/myco3d.ts`.**
   - It is built from lathe and sphere geometry with smooth normals and has no external model file.
   - Two skins, each with soft studio lighting (key, rim and fill), physically based materials, and
     a subtle sheen so the form reads:
     - **Black**: a near-black body with a faint violet rim light, as in the owner's screenshot.
     - **White**: a porcelain white body with soft grey shadows.
   - The eyes stay white on black and become charcoal on white, so they always read.
2. **Turned with the mouse.**
   - A `Myco3D` React component: drag rotates (yaw, plus pitch clamped to ±25°) and releases with
     inertia; double-click resets.
   - The keyboard arrow keys rotate it when it has focus.
   - It idles with a slow breathing scale and a blink, and stays still under reduced motion.
   - The render loop runs only while it is visible (IntersectionObserver) and stops when hidden.
3. **Where it appears.**
   - A preview route `?window=myco3d&skin=black|white` (full window, transparent or solid ground).
   - A Settings › 화면 card "myco 3D" with a skin toggle, Black or White (en/ko/ja).
4. **Videos.**
   - A committed script `app/scripts/myco3d-turntable.mjs` drives the preview with Playwright and
     records a 6 s, 360° turntable of each skin at 1080×1080: WebM (VP9) and an alpha-capable MOV
     where ffmpeg allows.
   - Output goes to `output/myco3d/`, which is gitignored like the rest of `output/`.
   - The owner gets the file paths.
5. **Budget.** The component lazy-loads into its own chunk, and nothing is added to the initial
   bundle.

## Acceptance

- vitest:
  - the model builder: part counts, bounds, the eye positions on the face;
  - the drag-to-rotation math: clamp, inertia decay;
  - skin materials.
- Browser, with 2× screenshots looked at:
  - the black and white skins front, three-quarter and side;
  - a drag rotating them;
  - keyboard rotation;
  - reduced motion;
  - side by side with the 2D poses, for silhouette match.
- The turntable videos are produced and played back (frame count and duration checked with
  ffprobe).
